## Legenda (copiar a partir daqui)

The problem did not start with a lack of tools.

It started with a request entering the business and nobody owning the next action.

We mapped:

→ where the request entered
→ what context was available
→ who had to decide
→ what could be handled by a system
→ where a human needed to take over

The result was not another generic automation.

It was a clearer operational path: trigger → owner → rule → next action → human.

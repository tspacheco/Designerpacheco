Have questions?

We got answers.

What makes hosting an event at V7 different from a traditional venue?

At V7, events happen in inspiring coworking hubs — not sterile halls. Our spaces are beautifully designed, filled with natural light and creative energy, and equipped with everything you need: 4K Smart TVs, sound systems, microphones, office supplies, and ultra-fast Wi-Fi.

We also provide hands-on support — from room setup and logistics to technical assistance during the event.

What’s included in the event space rental?

All essentials are included: projector or Smart TV, speakers, flipcharts, printer, Wi-Fi, coffee, tea, water, snacks, and restroom & kitchen amenities (yes, we even stock dental floss and hand cream).

Need more? We can connect you with trusted local suppliers for catering, pastries, photography, or extra equipment, so you can focus on your audience while we handle the details.

How do I get there and where can I park?

V7 Palas, Iași (7E Palas Street): Central location next to the Palace of Culture, with ~2,500 parking spaces in the Palas underground system.

What sets us apart

Live seafood,
every day.

We have our own tank where the day's seafood is kept alive until it reaches your plate. There is no fresher seafood than the kind that was alive minutes ago.

From lobster to spider crab, from prawns to spiny lobster — everything is chosen and prepared on the spot. That is what makes the difference.

European Recognition

Creating a Growth Funnel for Startups

At Rubik Hub, we design and run equity-free and fee-free programs for all startup stages – from idea to first traction, growth, and scaling. This creates a complete startup journey and a growth funnel that any ecosystem should have, guiding founders from idea to Series A.

We understand what each startup needs at every stage and personalize every program to meet their needs.

Restaurante JJ
You can order takeaway | Murteira

Experience wonderful delicious Portuguese cuisine – we serve you dishes that are freshly made and authentic. Welcome to the Restaurante JJ restaurant. We're happy to offer you our tasty dishes and give you an amazing experience. When the weather is good, you're more than welcome to sit in our relaxed outdoor seating area. For your comfort, we have air-conditioning so you can eat in peace, no matter what the weather is like.

The best restaurant in town with WiFi

Enjoy yourself and take your tastebuds on a journey with our Portuguese cuisine. We're always happy to serve you a delicious lunch or dinner in a relaxing atmosphere. Take advantage of our free WiFi to share your photos and experiences while you're with us.

Make use of our parking spaces

Please use our free parking spaces for diners. There's no need to reserve a table, just come on by when we're open – we look forward to having you! We accept VISA, MasterCard and debit card, although if you don't have your card with you, we also accept cash payments. We're also happy to prepare your order for takeaway, so you can enjoy it at home. We're open 6 days a week from 12:00 to 15:30 and 19:00 to 22:30, we're closed on Thursdays.

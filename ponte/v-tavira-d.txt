1928
Born on a bench in the Square

On a warm August night, a group of young tavirenses sat on a bench in Praça da República and founded the city’s oldest club.

almost a century later…

…the club’s table is still set, by the Gilão.

THE CLUB · SINCE 1928
Sport came first. Then, the table.

Gymnastics was the first section; football came right after; the nautical section put Tavira rowing on the Gilão. And between training, matches and regattas, there was always one place where the city met: the club’s table.

Today, the Ginásio’s restaurant is exactly that — Tavira’s living room, open to everyone, on Largo Dr. José Pires Padinha, right next to the market and the river.

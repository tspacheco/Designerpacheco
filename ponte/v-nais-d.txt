Shawarma in Iași, in short

Location, delivery, menu and opening hours: what people in Copou ask us.

Where is Nais Food in Iași?

We are in Copou, at Șoseaua Ștefan cel Mare și Sfânt 20, Copou, Iași. You can order online or take your shawarma away.

Do you deliver shawarma in Iași?

Yes. Order from the menu on naisfood.ro and we deliver in Iași. You can also call us on 0758 620 609.

What kind of shawarma do you make?

Shawarma wraps, plates and pitta, with chicken, beef or mixed. Meat prepared daily and house sauces.

What are your opening hours?

Monday – Friday: 08:00 – 19:00 · Saturday: 09:00 – 14:00 · Sunday: closed. Delivery: Monday – Friday, 09:00 – 17:00.

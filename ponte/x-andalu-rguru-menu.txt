Menu highlights from Andalu Gastrobar
The menu items below highlight top-rated and popular dishes based on real customer reviews for reference only, and may not accurately reflect the current restaurant offerings.
The price is considered high by some reviewers, but most agree that the quality of the food justifies the cost. The restaurant is described as offering good value for money, with the taste of Spain at a fair price according to the quality of the products.
Veal Pepito - this dish is a must-try, with reviewers praising its taste.
Beef Cheeks - prepared very well, this dish is highly recommended for meat lovers.
Octopus from the Tapas Menu - a standout dish that has been described as exceptional.
Paella with Sea Fruits - another exceptional dish, this paella is a favorite among customers.
Belgian Chocolate Cake - a perfect finish to your meal, this cake is described as amazing.
Nachos - despite a burnt bun, the nachos are praised as amazing.
Grilled Octopus with Potato Cream - highly recommended by a reviewer, this dish is worth trying.
Octopus a la Gallega - another octopus dish that has received high praise.
Shrimp Tacos with Goat Cheese Salad - a unique combination that has been recommended by reviewers.
Iberian Pork Secret - a tasty dish that meat lovers will enjoy.
White Sangria with Cava - a refreshing drink that pairs well with the dishes.
Classic Red Sangria - another delicious drink option, this classic sangria is highly recommended.
Sangria - both the classic red and white sangria with cava come highly recommended.

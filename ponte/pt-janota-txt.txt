I'm Janota.
I was born out of a love for our Portuguese food

- the kind that smells like grandma's house, but is served with pinta.

I am vain, yes. I have a tuxedo for details, beautiful glasses, well-composed dishes and long conversations at the table.

Discover what Janota has prepared for you - slowly, with gusto, overlooking the Ria.

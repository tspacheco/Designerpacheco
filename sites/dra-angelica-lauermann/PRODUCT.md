## Platform

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS, single file (Pacheco Studios engine: one `index.html` with CSS/JS inline, fonts embedded as base64, photos in `media/`, deploy by Netlify Drop or Hostinger). Decided by the agency's standing rules (CLAUDE.md / PLAYBOOK.md), not asked per site.

## Users

Adults in Mogi das Cruzes – SP (Vila São Paulo and surroundings) looking for a dentist they can trust: someone with pain or a broken tooth who needs to be seen, someone who has put off a check-up, older patients who need a prosthesis, people who want a whiter or more even smile. They arrive from Google Maps, from Instagram (@dra.angelicalauermann) or from a word-of-mouth recommendation, almost always on a phone, and their job is to decide "is she good and trustworthy?" and then message her on WhatsApp.

Secondary: the dentist herself, who will show the site to patients and judge it as a reflection of her work.

## Product Purpose

A one-page website for the practice of Dra. Angélica Lauermann Gomes, cirurgiã-dentista. It lets a new patient verify who she is (registration, experience, reputation), see what she treats, see where the practice is and when it is open, and start a conversation on WhatsApp in one tap. Success: more first contacts from people who found her online, and fewer "who is this dentist?" doubts before the first visit.

This is a first demo built by Pacheco Studios as part of a sales proposal; the dentist has not yet commissioned it.

## Product Principles

1. She is the product: every section should reinforce that the patient is treated by her, personally.
2. Verifiable over persuasive: registration number, address, real reputation and real photos before any adjective.
3. One tap to talk: WhatsApp is always within reach, with a message already started.
4. Honest gaps: anything not confirmed says "a confirmar" rather than guessing.
5. Calm for anxious patients: plain language about pain, first visits and what happens next.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Many visitors are older (prosthesis patients) and on mid-range phones: body text at least 17px, high contrast (AA minimum), large tap targets, no information carried by colour alone, respects reduced motion.

# Surface brief — sites/dra-angelica-lauermann/index.html

Scope: one-page demo website for Dra. Angélica Lauermann Gomes (cirurgiã-dentista, CRO-SP 139651, Mogi das Cruzes – SP). Visitor mode: **Persuade**.

Audience and job: a new patient on a phone (often anxious, sometimes older) who found her on Google Maps or Instagram and needs to decide "is she trustworthy?" and then message her.
Action: start a WhatsApp conversation with a pre-written message (only booking channel for now; no fake calendar).
Proof: her face and name, CRO-SP 139651, 6 years of practice (her bio), 5,0 on Google from 30 reviews (23/09/2026), her own photos, the real façade and address.
Content (confirmed with Tomás 24/09): full general-dentistry list and educational content authored at full fidelity, not waiting for the dentist; unknown facts (weekly hours, convênios, payment, prices) are never invented and point to "pergunte pelo WhatsApp". No prices, promotions or result promises (CFO). No review quotes.
Constraints: pt-BR, single-file engine, demo banner, LGPD note, no Livro de Reclamações.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the conversation a patient already has with a dentist on WhatsApp, held by the dentist herself: her contact card proves who she is, her messages answer what patients ask, and the composer is the call to action. It refuses the category default of a stock-smile hero, tooth-icon service cards and a floating "Agende" button.

OWN-WORLD: WhatsApp Business grammar rebuilt in her identity, never WhatsApp's own green or logo: deep forest-green header and her bubbles, white patient bubbles, pale sage wallpaper with an authored doodle pattern (line tooth, AL monogram, brush, sparkle), brass for ticks and stars, centered pill "system notices", quick-reply chips, a contact-info sheet. Funnel Display for names and questions, Funnel Sans for text, Pinyon Script only for the AL monogram.

STORY: The visitor meets her (face, name, CRO, rating), reads her own words, taps a question they have (treatments, first visit, fear of pain, where, when), gets her answer with her photos, and sends a WhatsApp message already written for the topic they chose.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Mobile: sticky forest contact header (avatar 44px, h1 name, "Cirurgiã-Dentista · CRO-SP 139651"), chat wallpaper, a "digitando…" indicator resolving into three of her bubbles (greeting, her bio line, portrait photo bubble), then a Google rating link-preview card; the composer bar is fixed at the bottom with the message pre-written and a send button. Desktop: two panes, left the "Dados do contato" sheet (large portrait, name, CRO, rating, address, phone, Instagram, WhatsApp button), right the conversation, same composer at the bottom of the right pane.

FORM: "A conversa com a Dra." — WhatsApp conversation, position 5 of 7 on the ordered list; seed key c9fab1ae (degraded roll, no challengers).

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: tapping a quick-reply chip or a treatment row scrolls to her answer and rewrites the composer text for that topic with a short typing animation; send opens wa.me with that exact text. Motion grammar: one entrance moment (typing indicator then bubbles) in the first viewport only; everything else is visible at rest.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

# 3. THE PROBLEMS WE SOLVE (dental)
- Missed calls: patients who call when reception is busy or closed and book at another clinic.
- Slow replies on WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, email and the website.
- Quotes that are never followed up ("I'll think about it" and then nothing).
- No-shows and last-minute cancellations with empty chairs.
- Old patients who have not come back for a year or more.
- Few Google reviews, and a clinic that does not appear when someone asks Google or an AI assistant for "the best dentist" nearby.
- The "Messy CRM": patient information scattered across WhatsApp, phone, email, paper and different software, so nobody has the full picture.
- The owner cannot see clearly what comes in and what turns into new patients.

# 4. THE AI TEAM: 12 AGENTS IN 5 TEAMS
Each agent has one task, a trigger and a metric.
Team 1, Front desk (nobody goes unanswered, at any hour):
- Voice receptionist: answers the phone 24/7, answers prices and hours from the clinic's approved list, and books the appointment. Metric: unanswered calls.
- Chat and WhatsApp agent: the same brain on the website and on WhatsApp; answers questions and takes the patient to a booking. Metric: minutes to first reply.
- Missed-call text-back: whoever called and was not answered gets a message within seconds, before calling the clinic next door. Metric: recovered calls.
Team 2, Conversion (nobody who asked for a price is forgotten):
- Quote follow-up: a 7-step sequence over 14 days (message, email with the price, concrete time slots, a call, objections, closing). Metric: quotes that become appointments.
- Email agent: sorts the inbox, drafts the reply and proposes free time slots; ignores advertising and invoices. Metric: emails answered the same day.
Team 3, Diary and retention (full chairs, old patients back):
- Confirmations and reminders: confirms the appointment, sends two reminders and offers a freed slot to the waiting list. Metric: no-shows per week.
- Patient reactivation: writes to patients absent for 11+ months on day 0, day 3 and day 7; anyone who books or says STOP leaves the sequence. Metric: patients who came back.
Team 4, Reputation and visibility (appear first on Google, the map and AI answers):
- Google reviews: after the visit asks how it went; 4–5 stars are invited to Google, 3 or less stay private so the clinic can solve it. Metric: new reviews per month.
- AI visibility report (AEO): shows whether the clinic appears when someone asks an AI assistant for the best dentist in their city. Metric: position in AI answers.
- Website audit: reads the live website (speed, SEO, sitemap) in about 30 seconds. Metric: performance score.
Team 5, Owner management (see everything without opening five apps):
- Clinic dashboard: requests, bookings, attendance and new patients in the last 30 days, with influenced revenue. Metric: request to new patient.
- Owner assistant: the owner asks by WhatsApp or Telegram for the day's diary, a patient's file, or creates a task for a stalled quote. Metric: tasks closed.
A clinic does not need all 12. We start with the one or two that solve the biggest bottleneck.

# 5. HOW WE WORK
1. We find the problem: a meeting with no commitment, then a study of where the clinic loses patients today (calls, messages, quotes, no-shows).
2. Onboarding: the clinic fills in an onboarding with its prices, hours, treatments, team, rules and the answers it wants to give. The agents are built on that onboarding, so they are ready to answer from day one. Nothing invented.
3. We switch the agents on. They answer based on the onboarding, and every exception goes to a person in the clinic. When something changes (a price, a schedule, a new treatment), the onboarding is updated and the agents follow it.
4. We prove the impact with a monthly dashboard: requests, bookings, attendance and new patients.
Usually the first agent is working in about a week.
Our principles: we start with the bottleneck, not the tool. Every system has a trigger, an owner, a rule and a handoff to a person. The clinic keeps its own accounts (WhatsApp, diary, Google, software) and its data. Every exception goes to a person. The agents only use information the clinic approved.

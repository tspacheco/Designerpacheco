At the table

It starts with tapas. It ends late.

Plates to share, a paella for two, a jug of cava sangria and a crema catalana to finish. Like a family-run place in Spain: Ioana and Daniel are on the floor.

01 · Tapas

Gambas al ajillo, pulpo a la gallega, hand-carved jamón ibérico.

Small plates for the middle of the table. The bread is for the prawn sauce.

02 · Paella

Paella for two, with rice from Valencia.

Seafood, black with prawns and squid, or chicken, rabbit and chorizo. Cooked to order; worth the wait.

03 · Bar

Cava sangria, Spanish wines, Flor de Sevilla gin and tonic.

A jug for the table, a Rioja or an Albariño by the bottle, a cocktail on the terrace.

04 · To finish

Crema catalana, San Sebastián cheesecake and one more coffee.

The part of the meal nobody hurries. Sobremesa, as the Spanish call it.

Our story

More than ten years in Madrid. Then home, to Copou.

We are Ioana and Daniel, a couple from Iași. We lived in Madrid for more than ten years, where Daniel worked in restaurants and Ioana for a healthcare company. The idea came in 2016, on a visit home. We moved back at the end of 2018 and opened Andalu in 2019.

The name came by chance, from a ceramics shop, during the renovation. Andalusia stayed in it. What did not come by chance: the ingredients from Spain, a chef who cooked in Spain and Andorra, and the fact that you will find us on the floor, at the tables, like the family-run places over there.

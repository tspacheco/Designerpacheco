Privacy

Your data, in short.

This site has no tracking cookies and sends nothing without an action from you. What we collect, why and for how long.

Who we are

What we collect and why

Booking on WhatsApp

The booking form sends nothing to us directly: it composes a message on your phone and opens WhatsApp. The details (name, date, time, number of guests) reach us only if you press "send" in WhatsApp. We use them to hold your table and keep them in the chat history, which we can delete on request.

Event quote request

The form sends your name, phone, event type, number of guests, date and message to the restaurant's email through our host's form service (Netlify). Basis: steps prior to a contract, at your request (art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We keep the request for the length of the discussion and, if no contract follows, for at most 12 months.

Google map

The map on the contact page loads only after you tap "Load the map". Only then does your browser communicate with Google, under Google's policy.

Fonts

Fonts load from Google Fonts; your browser sends Google your IP address when the page loads. Option: self-host the fonts, to decide before publishing.

Server logs

The host (Netlify) keeps short-term technical logs (IP address, page requested, time) for security.

Cookies

The site uses no cookies of its own and has no analytics or advertising tools. The only possible cookies are Google's, after you load the map.

Your rights

You can request access to your data, its rectification, erasure, restriction or portability, and object to processing, by writing to the address above. You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Romanian authority, ANSPDCP.

Events & catering

One long table, yours: at ours or at yours.

A 220 m² dining room, a 150 m² terrace, up to 100 guests. Or our team cooks paella and tapas at your venue. Tell us what you are celebrating and we reply with a proposal.

Three ways to do it

01 · Tapas

The sharing table

  • Tapas platters in the middle of the table
  • Jamón, cheeses, pimientos, bravas
  • Sangria Alegria by the jug
  • For birthdays and corporate events

price per person on request

02 · Paella

Paella for the whole table

  • Tapas to start
  • Paella cooked in big pans, in view
  • Crema catalana to finish
  • For christenings, small weddings, team lunches

price per person on request

03 · Catering

At your venue

  • Our team comes with the pans
  • Cold and hot tapas, paella on site
  • Tableware and staff on request
  • For weddings, garden parties, offices

quote after a visit

How it goes

01

You write to us

What you are celebrating, how many, when. On the form or on WhatsApp.

02

We reply

During opening hours, with a menu proposal and a price per person.

03

You taste

If you like, come for a meal and try the menu first.

04

We confirm

With a deposit and the final headcount, a few days before.

Request a quote

The form goes to the restaurant's email. We reply during opening hours.

01
220 m² indoors

02

Our story

A Portuguese "olá", a kitchen from Kathmandu.

This page is written with the owner. For now, what you can see and taste: the room, the oven and the menu.

The room

Prayer flags hanging from the ceiling, a golden Buddha by the door, the flag of Nepal on a wall. Simple tables, white cloths, an inner patio. It is small and it is family-run.

The kitchen

A tandoor, the clay oven where naan sticks to the wall and marinated chicken roasts at high heat. A spice blend made in-house, more than 15 of them, that goes into the slow-cooked chicken curry.

The street

Rua São João de Deus 21, in the centre of Grândola. The sign says "Welcome · Bem-vindo" and promises Nepali, Indian and Portuguese: there is a burger and a wrap for anyone avoiding spice.

Owners' names, opening year and the Nepal → Portugal journey: to be collected in a conversation with the restaurant.

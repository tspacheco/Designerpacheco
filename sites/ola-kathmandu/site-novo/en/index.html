Our story

From Kathmandu to Grândola, on a trail of spices.

Olá Kathmandu is a Nepali and Indian kitchen on a street in the centre of Grândola, Rua São João de Deus. In the dining room there are prayer flags, a golden Buddha and the flag of Nepal; in the kitchen, a tandoor and a spice blend made in-house.

The name joins the Portuguese "Olá" to the name of Nepal's capital, and that is what the place is: Nepali in the food, Alentejan in the neighbourhood. First-timers tend to leave saying it was a surprise. owners' story and opening year · to be confirmed

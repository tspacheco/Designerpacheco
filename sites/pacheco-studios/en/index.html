No mystery

So what is the AI behind it?

  1. It reads and writes text, like a very fast person who never gets tired. That’s all it does. It doesn’t “think” for you or decide anything on its own.
  2. The rules are ours and yours. We design what happens in each situation. You approve every text it can send. It picks from approved replies: it doesn’t make up prices or promises.
  3. It runs on your accounts. The WhatsApp is yours, the diary is yours, the Google profile is yours. Customer data stays where it already was.
  4. Everything is logged: every message sent, at what time, to whom and what they replied. You can read it all, whenever you like.
  5. When things go off-script, a person steps in. A question it doesn’t know, an upset customer, an odd request: it reaches you or us, with the whole conversation.

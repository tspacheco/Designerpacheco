How it works

Three steps, from basket to drawer.

  1. Load

    Put your laundry in the machine and choose the right programme.

  2. Start

    Pay at the machine and start it. Grab a coffee on Carol I while it runs.

  3. Dry and go

    Move everything to a dryer and take it home warm and dry.
